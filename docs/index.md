# Praxis

A workspace for agent-assisted software development. Work with conversations, files,
terminals, browser sessions and automations in the Canvas, while your backend runs
the agents on infrastructure you control.

[Get started](getting-started.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Self-hosting](SELF_HOSTING.md){ .md-button }

</div>

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-laptop: **Run it your way**

    ---

    Native on Linux, macOS or WSL 2, in one container, or as separate frontend and backend.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Installation](getting-started.md)

-   :material-cloud-outline: **Your VM is your personal cloud**

    ---

    Connect one frontend to several independent backends. Nothing is pooled or replicated.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Self-hosting](SELF_HOSTING.md)

-   :material-robot-outline: **Bring your agents and models**

    ---

    Choose your model provider and credentials, or connect external agents over ACP.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: ACP agents](ACP_AGENTS.md)

-   :material-code-braces: **Built on Praxis SDK**

    ---

    Agents, tools and the Agent Server come from the owned Praxis SDK at a frozen commit.
